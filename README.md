# Steel Plant Tracker - Vectorized Pro Version

This updated package includes the requested features:
1. **Full Page View**: A new "Full Page View" button that hides the sidebar and makes your map fill the entire screen dynamically.
2. **`F_D1.jpg` Integration**: The code is specifically configured to load your exact image filename (`F_D1.jpg`).
3. **Vectorized Zones & Custom Locations**: You can now draw polygons, rectangles, and custom location pins directly on the map to create zones dynamically!

## Instructions:
1. Extract the contents of this ZIP file.
2. Move your `F_D1.jpg` image into the exact same folder as the extracted `index.html`.
3. Open `index.html` in your web browser.

## How to draw Vectorized Zones:
- Look at the top-left corner of the map canvas. You will see a drawing toolbar.
- Click the **Polygon** (hexagon shape) or **Rectangle** (square shape) tool.
- Click on the map to trace a zone over your flow diagram.
- When you finish the shape, a popup will ask you to Name the zone (e.g., "Substation B").
- The zone is instantly saved to your database, vectorized, and appears in the sidebar dropdown so you can assign workers to it!
- To **Delete** a zone, click the Trash Can icon in the drawing toolbar, click the zone, and click "Save".
